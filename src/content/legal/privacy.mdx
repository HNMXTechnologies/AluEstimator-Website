This Privacy Policy explains how AluEstimator Technologies Pvt. Ltd. ("AluEstimator", "we", "us") handles information when you use our website at www.aluestimator.in and the AluEstimator app at app.aluestimator.in.

## What we collect

- **Account data** — names, phone numbers, email addresses and login details of the people in your firm who use AluEstimator.
- **Business data** — the information you enter to run your business: company profile and GSTIN, window templates, materials and prices, customers, estimates, quotations, invoices, payments, stock and coating records, and files you attach.
- **Usage data** — basic technical information such as sign-in times and actions recorded in the activity audit trail, used to keep the service secure and working.
- **Website enquiries** — the details you submit in the demo form (name, firm, city, phone, team size, current tool and message).

## Why we use it

We collect account, business and usage data **only to run the service** — to let you sign in, store and calculate your work, generate your documents, support you, and keep the service secure. We use demo-form details only to contact you about your demo and trial.

## Each firm's data is kept separate

Every firm (workspace) in AluEstimator is kept strictly separate. Staff only see the screens and data their role allows. Private business data is not stored in your device's offline cache.

## We never sell your data

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal or business data.

## Where data is stored

Your data is stored on secure cloud servers and backed up centrally. Access is protected by sign-in, optional two-factor authentication and session control.

## Website analytics

Our website uses cookie-free analytics to understand page visits. It does not use advertising cookies, so no cookie banner is needed.

## Export and deletion

You can ask us to export or delete your data at any time by writing to [support@aluestimator.in](mailto:support@aluestimator.in).
